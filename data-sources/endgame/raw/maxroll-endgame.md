**Key Equipment Notes:**
- Litany of Sable is a core item for Dread Claws, try to get it as soon as possible
- Footfalls of the Waning World allows you to teleport much more frequently
- Focus farming Footfalls with Attacks Reduce Evade Cooldown affix, then masterwork that stat
- Juggernaut's Aspect provides a powerful defensive upgrade (~30 to 35 percent DR with endgame setup)
- Defensive priorities: x1 Life on Hit (Weapon or Offhand), All Resist, Max Life, Armor

## Skill Bar Variants

**Primary Skill Rotation:**
1. Spam Dread Claws Enveloping Terror (primary damage)
2. Rampage Abyssal Titan (secondary AoE source)
3. Profane Sentinel (applies Vulnerable, triggers Dominion)
4. Command Laalish (applies Vulnerable, grants Shadowform, triggers Chains bonuses every 10 seconds)
5. Nether Step Evade (pulls summons, extends durations via Shadow Recall)
6. Sigil of Summons (boss alternative to Nether Step)
7. Metamorphosis Terror Demon (burst damage, generates Shadowform)
8. Dark Prison (automated by Prid aspect)
9. Command Fallen (starter only, replaced by Profane Sentinel once Dominion acquired)

**Leveling Bar Differences:** Starter version includes "Keep up Command Fallen to passively generate a lot of resources."
**Lv 70 Swap Moment:** Once you acquire Footfalls of the Waning World with an Attacks Reduce Evade Cooldown affix, swap Nether Step for Sigil of Summons.

## Resource Management Mechanics

**Shadowform Mechanic:**
- A buff gained and consumed by various Abyss abilities
- Main sources include Metamorphosis Terror Demon, Nether Step, Command Laalish, and Sigil of Subversion
- This build consumes them via movement and casting Dread Claws Enveloping Terror

**Summon Duration Management:** Nether Step pulls your summons to you and extends their durations with Shadow Recall, so you rarely have to recast them throughout a run.
**Dominion Trigger:** Profane Sentinel must be summoned every 12 seconds for the Paragon node.
