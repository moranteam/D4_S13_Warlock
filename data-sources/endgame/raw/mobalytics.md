# Additional Build Information
**Core Damage Mechanic:** Our Main Skill is Dread Claws. We utilize the Encircling Terror Dread Claws because it is much easier to hit and does higher damage.
**Damage Multiplier Summary:** The build achieves a ~15x damage multiplier through Berú of Horazon's Chains set interactions with 11 total Greater Demons.
**Strengths:** Insane Damage, Super Tanky, Fun and Fast.
**Weaknesses:** Clunky before Uniques.
