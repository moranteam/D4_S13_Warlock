# Respec Gates
**Level 15:** Unlock Soul Shards mechanic, acquire Mastermind Shard for Laalish and Abyss synergies.
**Level 30:** Unlock Fragments option, Blasphemous Fragment applies Hex via Rampage for amplified Abyss damage.
**Level 34:** Drop Hellion Sting and Sigil of Subversion, add Command Fallen (Fallen Rush) for resource generation and Dark Prison (Chain Aura) for defensive package.
**Level 40:** Drop Dark Prison, add Metamorphosis (Terror Demon) for Shadowform generation and utility. Dark Prison automation via Prid rune planned for later.

# Leveling Aspects
**Top Priority Imprints:**
1. Accelerating, offensive slot
2. Edgemaster's, offensive slot
3. Fortress, defensive slot
4. Deeper Shadows, offensive slot
5. Malevolence, offensive slot

**Recommended Slots:**
- Amulet: best offensive aspect (least replaced)
- Gloves: offensive aspects
- Chest, Pants: defensive aspects
- Boots: utility, mobility aspects
- Rings: resource generation aspects

# Leveling Gear Targets
**Priority Order:**
1. Weapon upgrade. Focus the most on upgrading your weapon, as its damage is the base for all further damage calculations.
2. Resistances (fill gaps)
3. Life pool
4. Armor
5. Damage modifiers (% Damage, Damage to Close, Vulnerable Damage based on conditions)

**General Strategy:** Temper low level gear liberally since tempering costs are manageable and results are impactful during leveling.

# Leveling Soul Shard and Fragment Choices
**Level 15 Mastermind Shard:**
- Unlocks Laalish demon
- Grants synergies for Abyss and Shadowform damage

**Level 30 Blasphemous Fragment:**
- Rampage applies Hex on targets
- Amplifies all subsequent Abyss damage significantly

# Lv 70 Swap Moment
As you approach level 70 with the Dread Claws Warlock, you unlock higher item power gear that represents a massive jump in character power. Refresh setup with new tempers and imprints. Paragon Levels unlock, enabling steady progression. Complete Pit Tier 10 to enter Torment 1 and begin endgame. Transition to endgame build variant or select alternative endgame build.

# Difficulty Progression While Leveling
**Recommendation:** Preferably, stay on Hard difficulty or above to benefit from the extra XP multipliers as long as your clear speed remains fast.
Adjust difficulty when content becomes trivial. If difficulty significantly slows clear speed, revert to lower tier.
**Post 70:** Complete Pit Tier 10 to unlock Torment 1 difficulty.

# Tempering and Masterworking During Leveling
**Tempering:** There's no point in holding on to your gold or materials for later. Use everything to speed up your journey to 70.
Temper gear early and liberally for impactful power scaling. Low level tempering is cost effective.
**Masterworking:** Not covered on this page.
