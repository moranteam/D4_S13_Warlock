# Leveling Aspects (priority order, offensive to defensive)
1. Insidious Aspect, Amulet
2. Edgemaster's Aspect, Ring 1
3. Aspect of Elemental Fate, Ring 2
4. Aspect of Peril, Weapon
5. Accelerating Aspect, Offhand
6. Remorseless Aspect, Gloves
7. Aspect of Impetus, Boots
8. Juggernaut's Aspect, Helm
9. Aspect of the Fortress, Chest
10. Aspect of Might, Pants

General: any Legendary Aspects that help Toughness, Healing, Cooldown, Abyss, or
Demonology Skills are great while leveling.

# Soul Shard And Fragment Timing
Mastermind Shard unlocked via the Warlock Class Quest. Blasphemous Fragment to apply
Hex via Command Fallen. Summon Laalish unlocked once Mastermind Shard obtained.

# Skill Point Allocation (upgrade to rank 15 in this order)
1. Dread Claws
2. Terror Swarm
3. Profane Sentinel
4. Command Fallen
5. Nether Step
Early focus: dump points into Dread Claws while waiting for unlocks.

NOTE: "Terror Swarm" naming does not match the canonical skill names used in our
data.js (Hellion Sting, Rampage). Treat as a source naming discrepancy, do not
overwrite canonical leveling skill data.

# Anything Distinctive
Mercenary: recommends Subo hired for Map Hack, Movement Speed, and Vulnerability.
Aldkin as reinforcement for damage reduction via Field of Languish. This is the first
canonical-tier source to explicitly recommend Subo, specifically for the leveling
phase. Playstyle: uses all buttons, high APM.
