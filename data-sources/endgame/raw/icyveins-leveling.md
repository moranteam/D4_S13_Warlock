# Transferable Facts (build agnostic, useful)
- Willpower scales all Warlock damage at 12.5 percent per 100 points. This is the
  single most useful number from this source.
- Soul Shard unlocks at Level 15. Options: Legion, Vanguard, Mastermind, Ritualist.
  No leveling preference declared.
- Difficulty: start on Hard for the smoothest fastest experience. Move up world tiers
  only when Helltides stop being a struggle. Quantity over quality.
- Weapon types for Warlock: Flails (shared with Paladin), 2H Swords/Maces/Axes, or
  1H Daggers/Maces/Axes with Focus offhand.
- Warlock has limited access to Dominance resource modifiers, so it is slower early
  and improves with gear.
