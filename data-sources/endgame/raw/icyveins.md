# Runes

## Chest Armor Runeword
- **Pairing:** Neo plus Prid
- **Effect:** Avoid taking damage for 2 seconds to generate offering. Prid invokes Dark Prison ability tethering enemies together.
- **Tier Ranking:** Essential for build fluidity

## Pants Runeword
- **Pairing:** Nagu plus Que
- **Effect:** Maintain at least 1 active Summon for 5 seconds, gaining Offering for each up to 5 summons. Invoke Earthen Bulwark to generate barrier.
- **Tier Ranking:** Key defensive mechanic

# Soul Shard and Fragment
**Recommended Soul Shard: Mastermind Shard.** Grants Summon Laalish Skill, allowing Recast Skills to not break Shadowform Stealth at cost of 2 stacks per cast. Provides x30 percent increased Abyss Skill damage while in Shadowform and 5 percent Movement Speed per stack.

**Alternative Fragment: Blasphemous Fragment.** Recast Skills apply Hex and increase damage enemies take from Abyss Demonology Skills by x20 percent.

**Synergy:** Both shards enhance offensive output and survivability through damage amplification and crowd control mechanics.

# Skill Bar Variants
**Final Endgame Skill Bar:**
1. Metamorphosis: enables casting Abyss skills without breaking Shadowform
2. Nether Step: movement and utility
3. Dread Claws: main damage output, consumes Shadowform stacks
4. Rampage: summons damage dealer, applies Hex
5. Command Fallen: maintains 3 summons for passive Dominance generation
6. Command Laalish: applies Vulnerable debuff on harder targets

# Boss Kill Rotations
**General Rotation Sequence:**
1. Summon 3 Command Fallen (maintain throughout)
2. Cast Metamorphosis to enable Shadowform generation
3. Summon Rampage
4. Spam Dread Claws on cooldown
5. Use Command Laalish against rares or bosses for Vulnerable
6. Use Nether Step for repositioning

# Resource Management Mechanics
**Shadowform:**
- Generated by Metamorphosis casting
- Consumed by Dread Claws (conjures additional claws per stack)
- Maximum stacks increased by Aspect of Deeper Shadows
- Provides x30 percent damage bonus to Abyss Skills while active

**Offering:**
- Generated by avoiding damage for 2 seconds (Neo plus Prid runeword)
- Generated by maintaining active summons (Nagu plus Que runeword: 1 per summon, max 5)

**Wrath:**
- Resource regenerated via chest affixes
- No specific cap mentioned

# Build Overview Summary
**Strengths:** One of the faster Warlock builds, quick burst damage output, relatively cheap to gear.
**Weaknesses:** Limited high Pit capabilities (110 plus), not the tankiest build.
**Core Mechanic:** Build operates with Rampage summon as primary damage delivery vehicle, while Dread Claws serves as main player controlled damage skill. All other abilities provide scaling buffs and debuffs.
