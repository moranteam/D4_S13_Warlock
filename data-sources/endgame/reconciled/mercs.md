## Canonical Hired Mercenary: Raheir

**Role:** Defensive front line, damage amplifier.
**Why for Dread Claws Mastermind:** Tankiness for the build's close range playstyle plus the Inspiration buff which provides a damage amplification to the player.

**Skill choices (Icy Veins canonical):**
1. Ground Slam (AoE crowd control opener)
2. Raheir's Aegis (defensive proc)
3. Bastion (sustained DR)
4. Inspiration (player damage amplification)

## Canonical Reinforcement Mercenary: Aldkin

**Role:** Damage reduction provider via passive trigger.
**Why:** Field of Languish on cast applies a damage reduction zone, which fires on any of Charlie's skill casts. Maxroll specifically calls out Aldkin reinforcement for this synergy.

**Key skill:** Field of Languish (triggers on player skill cast in combat, applies DR aura).

## Alternative Hired: Varyana

**Role:** Sustain through life heal, attack speed buff.
**Why pick:** Maxroll notes 1 percent Life Heal on hit plus Attack Speed buff. Mobalytics actually canonicalizes Varyana as hired.
**Key skill:** Bloodlust (triggers on any Skill cast in combat).

## Alternative Reinforcement: Varyana

When running Raheir hired (Maxroll canonical), Icy Veins recommends Varyana as the reinforcement instead of Aldkin. Trade off: more Attack Speed and sustain (Varyana) vs more damage reduction (Aldkin).
