## Pit Progression Tiers

### Recommended Pit Pushing By Power Level
- Pit 10: unlocks Torment 1 immediately at Lv 70 ding
- Pit 20 to 35: glyph leveling sweet spot for glyphs to level 15 to 21
- Pit 35 to 65: glyph leveling 21 to 35
- Pit 65 to 80: glyph leveling 35 to 51, push for Mythic farm
- Pit 80 to 100: optimization push, build can do Pit 100 per Icy Veins
- Pit 100 plus: stretch goal, build is not optimized for Pit 110 plus pushes per Icy Veins

### Glyph Upgrade Pit Tier Thresholds
Sources do not enumerate per glyph Pit tier requirements. General D4 mechanic: each glyph upgrade past level 15 requires clearing a Pit tier equal to or slightly above the current glyph level minus 5.

Confidence: HIGH on the Pit 100 ceiling (Icy Veins explicit). MEDIUM on tier by tier glyph progression (community standard).
