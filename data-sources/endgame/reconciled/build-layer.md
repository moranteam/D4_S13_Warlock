## Runes

### Canonical Runewords
- **Chest:** Neo plus Prid. Neo generates Offering after 2 seconds without taking damage. Prid invokes Dark Prison Chain Aura. Lets you drop Dark Prison from the skill bar after the Lv 40 respec. Confidence HIGH.
- **Pants:** Nagu plus Que. Nagu generates Offering by maintaining at least 1 active Summon for 5 seconds, up to 5 summons (500 max). Que invokes Earthen Bulwark Barrier. Confidence HIGH.

### Alternative Combos
- **Cir plus Ceh:** S tier. Cir on Crit generates Offering. Ceh invokes Vulnerable application plus damage ramp. Use if not running Neo plus Prid for chest. Confidence HIGH.
- **Cir plus Que:** A tier. Same Cir generator, Que invokes 45 percent Barrier every 3 seconds. Mobalytics primary chest pair variant. Confidence HIGH.
- **Igni plus Prid:** S tier alternative chest pair. Igni stores Offering every 0.3 seconds up to 500. Prid invokes Dark Prison. Use if you want on demand Offering store instead of avoid damage trigger. Confidence HIGH.

### Drop Sources
Runes drop from Helltide chests, Whisper caches, and certain Nightmare Dungeon bosses. Specific drop tables not enumerated in surveyed sources. Confidence LOW on individual rune farm spots.

## Soul Shard and Fragment (locked combo)

### Soul Shard: Mastermind
Grants Summon Laalish skill. Recast Skills do not break Shadowform Stealth at cost of 2 stacks per cast. Provides x30 percent increased Abyss Skill damage while in Shadowform plus 5 percent Movement Speed per stack.

### Fragment: Blasphemous
Recast Skills (Rampage, etc.) apply Hex on targets. Increases damage enemies take from Abyss Demonology Skills by x20 percent.

### Synergy
Mastermind Shard generates Shadowform stacks via Recast skills. Blasphemous Fragment makes the Recast cycle also apply Hex, which amplifies all subsequent Abyss damage from Dread Claws, Rampage, and Profane Sentinel. Stacked multipliers compound for the canonical x15 damage figure per Mobalytics.

### Confidence
HIGH. All 3 sources agree on Mastermind plus Blasphemous as the locked combo.

## Paragon Board Rotation

Board rush setup. Take legendary nodes immediately on each board, defer glyph sockets until rotation. Respec to full setup after passing 200 Paragon.

Full board setup with all glyph sockets activated, all rare nodes for stat targets, legendary nodes locked in.

## Skill Bar Variants

### Final Endgame Bar (Maxroll canonical, 6 slots)
1. **Dread Claws: Encircling Terror** (primary spam, consumes Shadowform stacks for extra claws)
2. **Rampage: Abyssal Titan** (Greater Demon, applies Hex via Blasphemous Fragment)
3. **Profane Sentinel** (applies Vulnerable, triggers Dominion paragon node every 12 seconds, replaces Command Fallen once Dominion is acquired)
4. **Command Laalish** (applies Vulnerable on hard targets, grants Shadowform, procs Chains of Horazon every 10 seconds)

## Resource Management Mechanics

### Wrath Generation Sources (Mobalytics enumerated)
1. Ring of Starless Skies (unique item passive)
2. Dominion class mechanic (Profane Sentinel paragon node trigger every 12 seconds)
3. Wrath Regeneration affix on chest
4. Wrath Paragon Glyph node
5. Unbound Paragon Glyph

**Optimal kit:** Ring of Starless Skies plus Dominion plus Unbound plus 1x Wrath Regeneration affix keeps Wrath topped up regardless of Attack Speed.

### Offering Mechanics
- Generated by Neo plus Prid (avoid damage 2 sec)
- Generated by Nagu plus Que (active summons, 1 per summon up to 5, max 500)
- Consumed by Prid (Dark Prison cast) and Que (Earthen Bulwark Barrier)

### Shadowform Stack Maintenance
**Generators (4 sources):**
- Metamorphosis: Terror Demon generates 4 stacks per second while active
- Nether Step grants 4 stacks per cast
- Command Laalish generates stacks on cast
- Sigil of Subversion generates stacks

**Consumers:**
- Dread Claws: Encircling Terror consumes stacks to spawn extra claws
- Movement (Evade) consumes stacks

**Maximum:** Increased by Aspect of Deeper Shadows.
**Effect while active:** x30 percent damage to Abyss Skills, 5 percent Movement Speed per stack.

### Boss Kill Rotation (Icy Veins canonical)
1. Summon 3 Command Fallen at start (maintain throughout)
2. Cast Metamorphosis to enable Shadowform generation
3. Summon Rampage on the boss
4. Spam Dread Claws on cooldown
5. Use Command Laalish for Vulnerable on rares or bosses
6. Use Nether Step for repositioning

### Confidence
HIGH on Shadowform sources and consumers. HIGH on Wrath kit. MEDIUM on the exact boss rotation order (Icy Veins is the only source with explicit steps).
